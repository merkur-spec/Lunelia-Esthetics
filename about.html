About Us

Lunelia Esthetics is an owner operated studio specializing in fast, gentle hard wax services and results driven corrective skincare.

Every appointment is one on one, backed by over 3 years of speed waxing experience and training in corrective treatments. Appointments move fast, results are clean, and if you're dealing with breakouts, uneven tone, texture, or ingrowns, you'll get an honest look at what your skin needs. Master level licensed, IMAGE Skincare certified, and DermaPlanePro certified.
